How Just Keep Talking started

I’ve been studying Spanish since I was young, always with very traditional methods. Even after years of grammar drills and memorization, I never felt truly confident speaking. Everything changed during an exchange in Costa Rica. Real conversations and real connections helped my Spanish grow. In 2021, I moved here and began Just Keep Talking: personalized online lessons in a space where students want to speak and feel safe to make mistakes.

Audrey

Founder & teacher

Hello, hello! I'm Audrey, and I'm originally from Michigan, USA. I moved to Costa Rica in 2021 after falling in love with the country during a six-month exchange program in 2018. I love the people, nature, food, and culture, and I'm so happy to call Costa Rica home!

I studied Spanish, education, and English linguistics at university, so I'm kind of a grammar nerd! I've spent years both teaching and learning languages, so I know how challenging and rewarding the process can be. One of my favorite parts of teaching is helping students build confidence and discover new opportunities through English.

Outside of the classroom, I love traveling, reading, cooking, hiking, and spending time with friends and family. I can't wait to meet you!
