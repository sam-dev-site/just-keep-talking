## Academy and offer

- Just Keep Talking is a virtual language academy.
- It offers private and small-group classes for students of all levels throughout the Americas.
- Lessons are designed around individual goals, learning styles, and interests rather than a generic curriculum.
- The methodology centers on natural conversation and real-life communication while strengthening grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, and writing.
- The intended environment is supportive, comfortable, motivating, and safe for mistakes.
- The stated mission is to help students reach personal and professional goals through authentic, conversation-based English learning for work, travel, academics, and daily life.

## Values

- Authentic learning through real conversations and situations.
- Personalized lessons based on each student's goals and learning style.
- A supportive community that builds comfort and confidence.
- Educational excellence through certified teachers and skill development.
- Meaningful, enjoyable, interactive connection and engagement.

## 2027 enrollment payment and attendance policy

Effective date: January 4, 2027.

### Enrollment and tuition

- Tuition is a fixed monthly amount, not hourly billing.
- Tuition reserves a recurring weekly schedule and is annualized across the academic calendar.
- Monthly tuition does not change because a month has five class weeks, scheduled holidays, or academy breaks.
- Enrollment renews monthly. Once a month begins, the student is responsible for that full month; mid-month withdrawal does not create a refund or prorated credit.
- A new student's first month may be prorated when they begin after the start of the month.
- One-time enrollment fees: adults ₡15,000; kids ₡10,000.

### Published academy closures

- March 21-28: Semana Santa break.
- April 11: Juan Santamaría Day.
- May 1: Labor Day.
- July 25: Annexation of Guanacaste / Partido de Nicoya.
- August 15: Mother's Day.
- September 15: Independence Day.
- December 22-31: Christmas and New Year break.

Classes during published closures are not rescheduled or credited because the closures are built into annualized tuition.

### Attendance and rescheduling

- At least eight hours' notice is required for a student-requested reschedule.
- Rescheduling is subject to teacher availability.
- A make-up must occur within 14 days of the original class.
- Late cancellations and no-shows are lost and are not rescheduled or credited.
- Rescheduling opportunities expire after 14 days and do not carry forward.
- Monthly allowance: one reschedule for one weekly session, two for two, three for three, and four for four.
- An individual group member who misses a class held as scheduled does not receive an individual make-up or credit.
- If JKT or a teacher cancels, JKT will try to reschedule within 14 days; if no workable time is found, the student receives a credit toward the following month.

### Extended absences

- Planned requests should be made at least 14 days in advance when possible.
- Standard extended-absence adjustments may apply for up to two adjusted months per calendar year.
- Two consecutive weeks: 25% discount on that month's regular tuition.
- Three or more consecutive weeks: 50% discount on that month's regular tuition.
- Full month with the schedule held: 50% schedule-hold tuition.
- Full month with the schedule released: no tuition, but the teacher and time are not guaranteed on return.
- Missed classes covered by an adjusted absence do not produce make-ups, carryovers, or credits.
- Serious exceptional circumstances may receive a case-by-case pause, adjustment, credit, or accommodation.

### Payment and withdrawal

- Standard single-payment window: the 1st through 7th of each month.
- Standard two-installment windows: the 1st through 7th and the 15th through 21st.
- An approved alternative recurring date may be used.
- Classes may be paused when payment remains overdue. Missed classes during a nonpayment pause are not rescheduled, credited, or deducted from tuition.
- Enrollment renews automatically each calendar month unless withdrawal is communicated before the next month begins. One week's notice is requested when possible.
- Routine class carryovers are not used. Credits are limited to approved absence adjustments, referral or promotional credits, billing corrections, exceptional circumstances, or an academy/teacher cancellation that cannot be rescheduled.
