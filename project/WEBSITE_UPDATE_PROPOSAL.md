# Proposed website updates from the 2027 program materials

## Design recommendation added September 17 2026

This recommendation develops the earlier content inventory into a visitor experience. It is based on the supplied notes, pricing graphics, policy extracts, and the local website files `site/index.html`, `site/script.js`, and `site/style.css`. The deployed website was not independently inspected in this review. Everything in this section is a proposal, not an implemented change.

### Overall direction

Evolve the adult private-class landing page into a small academy website with three main destinations: Home, Classes and pricing, and Our story and team. Keep Policies and Teach with us accessible from the footer. Keep WhatsApp prominent throughout.

Retain the established navy, blue, cyan, lime, warm background, conversational tone, and real photography. Use the new material to make the offer easier to understand and the people behind it more visible. Avoid adding every supplied paragraph to the homepage.

### Homepage sequence

| Position | Proposed content | Design and purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Inclusive introduction | A clear English-learning promise, an explicit adults-and-kids description, one genuine teaching photo, and a WhatsApp action. |
| 2 | Two audience paths | Side-by-side Adults and For your child cards; stacked on phones. Each leads to the appropriate classes and pricing view. |
| 3 | Teaching approach | Three concise ideas: lessons shaped around goals, meaningful conversation, and a supportive teacher relationship. |
| 4 | Audrey's story | Portrait beside a short account of learning Spanish and discovering confidence through real conversation; link to the full story. |
| 5 | Getting started | The existing three-step process, revised to include choosing an appropriate format and schedule with JKT. |
| 6 | Meet the teachers | A compact team preview with a link to all profiles. |
| 7 | Practical questions and next action | Brief FAQs, a visible link to prices, and a closing WhatsApp invitation. |

This replaces the earlier suggestion of four equally prominent homepage program cards. Visitors first identify who the classes are for, then compare private and group learning. That reduces the number of decisions at the entrance.

The existing headline, “You know more English than you're saying,” remains useful on the adult section. It assumes prior knowledge and should not introduce the entire academy now that beginners and children are included.

Proposed homepage copy direction: “Build the confidence to speak.” Support it with “Personalized online English classes for adults and kids, with private and small-group options.” These are draft editorial recommendations, not a newly approved brand tagline. Spanish classes should receive their own clear offer only after availability is confirmed.

### Classes and pricing experience

Use one focused page with Adults and Kids choices. Within the selected audience, show Private and Small group. Show group size only when Small group is selected. Keep an independent CRC / USD control visible above the prices; language and currency must not be coupled.

Show four plan cards for the selected combination. Each card should contain the plan name, weekly time, monthly price, and a short WhatsApp action. Put the applicable one-time enrollment fee directly beside or below the plans. A visitor should be able to understand the total recurring and initial costs without opening a policy document.

Use the newest matching non-phase graphic as the source for both prices and any downloadable artwork. New prices are often higher, as the owner noted, but price alone does not establish recency or resolve a disagreement in plan duration. Do not select a larger number across different programs as a substitute for identifying the matching source.

Adult private cards use Essential, Standard, Intensive, and Immersion at ₡65,000 / ₡125,000 / ₡183,000 / ₡238,000 monthly, or $150 / $285 / $420 / $545. Remove the old fixed class counts and per-class savings. Monthly calendar variation means those labels can misrepresent what the customer purchases.

The graphic colors can inspire small speech-bubble accents. Set price and schedule text in high-contrast dark type on light surfaces; do not reproduce white text on lime as the main reading treatment. Keep all prices available as real page text. On phones, use stacked cards with compact spacing, not a horizontally scrolling price table.

Carry audience, format, group size, plan, and currency into the WhatsApp message. This lets JKT answer the actual inquiry without asking the visitor to repeat their selection. Treat selections as an inquiry, not a reservation or availability guarantee.

Group graphics do not explicitly identify whether a displayed amount is per student or for the entire group. Confirm that unit before adding a public “per student” label. Also confirm whether people bring their own group or can join an existing one; this determines the appropriate group call to action.

### Story and team

Give Audrey's story the most space on the About page: the problem she experienced as a learner, the change brought by real conversation in Costa Rica, and the academy she began in 2021. Use approximately 150–220 words plus a confirmed portrait. A compact homepage version should be about 50–70 words.

Present all six teacher profiles in a consistent grid: portrait, name, teaching background, and one personal detail. Aim for 50–80 words each. Preserve useful distinctions, such as Iain's interest in beginner adults and Cristian's Spanish-teaching experience. Do not infer nationality, qualifications, or specialty from a name or portrait, and do not offer teacher booking without an actual availability process.

Translate the values into three concrete explanations of class experience rather than a large separate mission-and-values section. Keep the full source text in project context. Use the supplied certification statement carefully; no invented certification logos, graduate counts, testimonials, or guaranteed outcomes.

### Visual refinement

- Keep the current palette and genuine photography, with a consistent crop and treatment across teacher portraits.
- Use speech-bubble geometry sparingly for section accents, connecting the expanded design to the new pricing materials.
- Reduce the repeated oversized headings and large empty vertical spaces as the site gains content. Give the hero and founder story emphasis; keep pricing and FAQs more compact.
- Use dark text on cyan and lime surfaces and white text on navy after checking actual contrast.
- Keep adults and kids within the same visual identity. Differentiate them through copy and appropriate photographs rather than creating a second cartoon-style brand.
- Preserve the existing no-default-highlight pricing treatment. Avoid an unsupported “most popular” label.
- Keep profile cards visible in a grid rather than hiding them in an automatic carousel.

### Navigation and bilingual behavior

Primary navigation: Classes and pricing, Our approach (homepage anchor), Our story and team, plus a distinct WhatsApp button and EN / ES control. Footer: Policies, Teach with us, email, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

Replace the current mobile behavior that simply hides navigation below 980px with an accessible menu. Switching languages should keep visitors on the equivalent page and preserve their program and currency selections. Dedicated English and Spanish page URLs are recommended for sharing and search discovery as the site expands. They can still be delivered as a small static website.

Use the friendlier primary button “Let's talk on WhatsApp” / “Conversemos por WhatsApp.” Explain the intake conversation in supporting text. On pricing cards, use “Ask about this plan” / “Consulta por este plan.” Recruitment should use its own application message.

### Policies and secondary audiences

Use a readable bilingual policy page with a short contents list, clear headings, and the effective date. The supplied policy begins January 4, 2027, which is in the future at the time of this review. If published sooner, label it as the upcoming 2027 policy; do not imply its attendance and payment terms already apply. The owner's confirmation that the new graphics contain the latest prices is separate from this policy effective date.

The homepage FAQ should focus on fit and enrollment: beginner levels, online delivery, adults and kids, private versus group, and how to get started. Put detailed absence and payment rules on the policy page, with a short relevant summary beside prices once timing is confirmed.

Teach with us should have a concise benefits section, expectations, and an email or WhatsApp application action. Curriculum sales need a defined product before they deserve a navigation item or purchase flow. The homepage should continue to prioritize prospective students and parents.

### Priority and review criteria

Implement the accurate offer and adult pricing first, then the audience-based pricing experience, founder/team content, and supporting pages. Kids duration discrepancies block only the affected price cards. Unresolved policy differences block only publication of those terms; they need not delay the other design work.

The result should let an adult or parent find the appropriate offer, understand its weekly time and monthly cost, see who teaches, and contact JKT with their selection. Check those journeys in both languages on phone and desktop, including keyboard operation. Verify that no existing-student phase graphic, superseded price, invented group-billing unit, or prematurely effective 2027 policy language appears.

## Recommendation

Update the website in two stages. First, correct the public offer and adult-private pricing so the current site is not materially outdated. Second, expand the site to support adults, kids, groups, the academy story, policies, and teacher recruitment after the kids-pricing discrepancies are resolved.

No `Phase 1` or `Phase 2` language or pricing should appear. Those graphics concern existing students, while the website targets prospective students.

## Priority 0 corrections

### Replace the current adult private prices

The live-site source currently shows ₡36,000, ₡68,000, ₡96,000, and ₡120,000. Replace those values with the latest adult-private monthly prices:

- Essential, 1 hour weekly: ₡65,000 or $150.
- Standard, 2 hours weekly: ₡125,000 or $285.
- Intensive, 3 hours weekly: ₡183,000 or $420.
- Immersion, 4 hours weekly: ₡238,000 or $545.
- Adult enrollment: ₡15,000 or $35.

Remove the current per-hour and savings calculations unless JKT supplies approved equivalents for the new monthly tuition model. The 2027 policy explicitly describes fixed monthly tuition rather than hourly billing.

### Change the price presentation

Keep prices as accessible HTML rather than replacing the section with image-only pricing graphics. Use the newest graphics as the authoritative source and optional share/download assets. Recommended controls:

- Adult or kids.
- Private or group.
- Group size: two, three, or four-plus.
- CRC or USD.

Every displayed amount should say `per month`, and enrollment fees should be clearly separated as one-time fees.

### Update the offer language

The hero and metadata currently describe only private one-to-one English classes. Revise them to reflect the broader academy:

- Virtual language academy.
- Private and small-group classes.
- Adults and kids.
- Students of all levels across the Americas.
- Personalized, conversation-led lessons.

Keep the present confidence and real-conversation positioning because it remains consistent with the new material.

## Priority 1 additions

### Programs and pricing page

Create a dedicated programs page instead of forcing all adult, kids, private, group, and currency combinations into the home page. The home page can show four clear entry cards:

- Adult private.
- Adult group.
- Kids private.
- Kids group.

Each card should open the relevant pricing view and end with a WhatsApp intake action. Do not publish the kids price selector until the schedule labels and kids-private CRC/USD mismatch are confirmed.

### About and founder section

Add a concise founder story explaining Audrey's experience learning Spanish, the Costa Rica exchange, and why JKT focuses on conversation and confidence. This adds credibility and gives the existing methodology a clear origin.

Recommended supporting content:

- Mission statement.
- A short values list rather than all six values at full length.
- Time-sensitive growth claims only after reconfirmation.

### Teacher section

Add a team section with consistent short biographies, country background, teaching focus, and a personal detail. Avoid unequal biography lengths. Confirm photos and final titles before publication.

Cristian's Spanish-teaching biography suggests JKT may offer Spanish as well as English. If that is an active public offer, give it a clear program path; otherwise, do not let one biography imply an unavailable service.

### Policies page

Add a bilingual `Policies` page effective January 4, 2027, linked from pricing and the footer. Keep the full policy available there and summarize only the points a prospective student needs during enrollment:

- Fixed monthly tuition and recurring schedule.
- First-month proration for new students.
- Eight-hour rescheduling notice.
- Make-ups within 14 days and subject to availability.
- Late cancellations and no-shows are lost.
- Monthly renewal and withdrawal before the next month.
- One-time adult and kids enrollment fees.

Do not crowd the main marketing page with the full calendar, absence discount matrix, overdue-payment rules, or administrative exceptions.

### Contact options

Keep WhatsApp as the primary conversion action. Add email and Instagram in the footer and contact area:

- `justkeeptalkingcr@gmail.com`
- `@justkeeptalkingcr`

### Teacher recruitment

Create a separate `Teach with us` page or footer link for prospective teachers. Include the supplied benefits and expectations in edited, scannable form, then direct applicants to WhatsApp or email. This audience should not compete with student enrollment in the main hero.

### Curriculum interest

Treat curriculum sales as a separate future path. Add no purchase claims or checkout until the product, license, audience, deliverables, support, pricing, and terms are defined. A simple `Interested in JKT curriculum? Contact us` prompt is safe after JKT confirms it wants public inquiries.

## Recommended information architecture

- Home: academy promise, audiences, method, founder preview, teacher preview, primary WhatsApp action.
- Programs and pricing: adult/kids, private/group, CRC/USD.
- About: story, mission, values, full team.
- Policies: bilingual 2027 policy and concise enrollment summary.
- Teach with us: benefits, expectations, and application contact.

This can remain a static site. No account system, payment system, booking system, or backend is justified by the supplied requirements.

## Content and design rules

- Use the newest matching graphics and prices; older matching materials are superseded.
- Preserve old originals in the source archive rather than deleting them.
- Do not use phased-pricing graphics or phase language publicly.
- Do not make pricing image-only. The content must remain readable, selectable, responsive, and accessible.
- Label CRC and USD explicitly. Do not imply that exchange-rate conversion is automatic.
- Show the policy effective date.
- Keep English and Spanish content equivalent.
- Correct obvious copy errors while preserving meaning.
- Avoid publishing operational or date-sensitive claims without a freshness check.
