About us

We are a virtual language academy offering private and small-group classes for students of all levels throughout the Americas. Every learner is unique, and so are our lessons. Instead of a generic curriculum, we design each class around individual goals, learning styles, and personal interests.

Our methodology is rooted in natural, conversational learning. Students gain confidence speaking in real-life situations while strengthening essential skills such as grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, and writing.

Most importantly, we believe that learning happens best in a supportive environment. Our teachers build genuine connections with students, creating a space where they feel comfortable, motivated, and inspired to grow.

Our mission 
We empower students to achieve their personal and professional goals through authentic, conversation-based English learning. Our mission is to help every student communicate confidently in real-world situations — whether for work, travel, academics, or daily life.

Our values 
Authentic Learning – Real conversations, real situations, real results.
Personalized Approach – Every lesson is tailored to individual goals and learning styles.
Supportive Community – We prioritize comfort and confidence so students feel safe to speak and make mistakes.
Excellence in Education – Certified teachers who combine engaging conversation with strong skill development.
Connection & Engagement – Learning should feel meaningful, enjoyable, and interactive.
Lifelong Growth – We encourage curiosity and continuous learning beyond the classroom.





Our History 
My name is Audrey, and I’m the founder of Just Keep Talking. I’d love to share how this project began.
I’ve been studying Spanish since I was young — in school, high school, and university — always with very traditional methods. Even after years of grammar drills and memorization, I never felt truly confident speaking.
Everything changed when I participated in an exchange program in Costa Rica. Being immersed in real conversations, surrounded by people and culture, I finally felt like I could speak the language.
That experience changed the way I saw language learning. So after returning to the U.S., I continued studying education with a focus on teaching Spanish and English as a second language. I dreamed of creating a program that felt more human — one centered on meaningful conversation, real connection, and learning through authentic interaction, rather than rigid textbooks.
In 2021, I moved to Costa Rica to bring that dream to life.
I started teaching virtual classes, creating my own lessons, and shaping a methodology that focused on personalized learning, comfort, and confidence. The goal was simple: create a learning environment where students want to speak and feel safe to make mistakes.
Over time, what began as one teacher with a laptop grew into a full team and a thriving community. Today, Just Keep Talking is made up of several certified teachers and nearly 100 students across the Americas — each with their own unique goals, stories, and successes.
And this is just the beginning.
