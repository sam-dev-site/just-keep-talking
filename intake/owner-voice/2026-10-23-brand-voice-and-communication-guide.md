# Just Keep Talking — Brand Voice & Communication Guide

Source: Owner-facing summary of brand voice and messaging guidelines developed from conversations about the website, Instagram content, marketing strategy, and founder communication. Received 23 October 2026. Archive as-is; do not rewrite this file into “better” English.

---

This is a summary of the brand voice and messaging guidelines we developed for Just Keep Talking, based on our previous conversations about your website, Instagram content, marketing strategy, and how you want to communicate as the founder.

## 1. Our brand personality

Just Keep Talking should sound like a real person who genuinely cares about language learning, understands the challenges students face, and knows how to help them.

Our voice is:

- Warm and welcoming: Friendly, approachable, and encouraging without being overly sentimental.
- Conversational and natural: We write the way a thoughtful, knowledgeable person would actually speak.
- Knowledgeable but accessible: We demonstrate teaching expertise without sounding academic, overly technical, or intimidating.
- Authentic and personal: Real experiences, observations, and stories are more valuable than generic motivational messages.
- Thoughtful and observant: We notice the little things about language, communication, culture, and learning that people can relate to.
- Confident but not pushy: We believe in our methodology and communicate its value without exaggerating or constantly selling.

The overall feeling: A knowledgeable, friendly language teacher having a genuine conversation with you—not a company trying to convince you to buy something.

## 2. Our central message

Learn a language by using it.

Language is more than grammar rules, vocabulary lists, or passing exams. It's about connecting with people, expressing yourself, sharing experiences, and participating in real conversations.

Our communication should consistently reflect these ideas:

- Conversation is at the center of learning, but it is supported by structure, guidance, and meaningful feedback.
- Every student has different goals, challenges, interests, and experiences. Learning should reflect that.
- Mistakes are a natural and necessary part of the learning process.
- Confidence develops through practice, encouragement, and experience.
- Language learning is about more than fluency. It's about connection, independence, culture, and being able to express who you are.

We don't position grammar as the enemy or suggest that students can learn simply by chatting. Our approach balances communication, accuracy, structure, and personalized support.

## 3. How we write

Do more of this | Avoid this
---|---
Natural, everyday language | Corporate or overly polished language
Specific examples and real situations | Vague motivational statements
Thoughtful observations | Generic language-learning tips
Short, clear sentences | Unnecessarily long explanations
Genuine questions | Forced engagement tactics
Encouragement grounded in reality | Exaggerated promises
Stories and personal experiences | Manufactured testimonials or scenarios presented as real
Gentle, relevant invitations | Aggressive sales language

### Examples of the difference

TOO GENERIC / SALESY

"Unlock your full potential and master English with our innovative methodology!"

MORE LIKE JUST KEEP TALKING

"You might understand a lot of English, but when it's time to speak, finding the words can feel completely different. That's something we work on together."

TOO CHEESY

"Don't let fear stop you from achieving your dreams! Your fluency journey starts today! 🚀✨"

MORE LIKE JUST KEEP TALKING

"You don't need to speak perfectly to have a good conversation. Sometimes the most important thing is being willing to try."

## 4. How the voice changes across platforms

The personality stays consistent, but the presentation changes depending on where we're communicating.

### Website — Warm, clear, and professionally confident

The website should feel welcoming while establishing credibility. We explain our approach, programs, certified teachers, personalized plans, and student experience in clear, meaningful language.

Primarily use "Just Keep Talking," "we," and "our," rather than the abbreviation "JKT."

The goal is for visitors to think: They understand what I need, they know what they're doing, and I would feel comfortable learning here.

### Instagram Reels — Personal, insightful, and natural

Reels should sound like you speaking to someone, not reading a marketing script. Focus on one relatable situation, interesting observation, or useful teaching insight.

Avoid overly rehearsed delivery, forced trends, or content that feels like it's trying too hard to go viral.

### Instagram Carousels — Thoughtful, educational, and easy to follow

Content should be organized, visually simple, and genuinely useful. Each slide should contribute something meaningful.

Captions should add an extra thought or personal perspective rather than repeat the slides. End with a natural question when it encourages meaningful conversation.

### Instagram Stories — Casual, spontaneous, and human

This is where we can be the most informal. Share everyday teaching moments, behind-the-scenes experiences, what you're working on, interesting student questions (without identifying students), and little observations.

Stories don't always need a polished message or an educational takeaway. Sometimes simply showing the person behind the academy is enough.

### WhatsApp, Emails & Announcements — Friendly, helpful, and direct

Communicate clearly, with warmth and professionalism. Messages should be easy to read and make the next step obvious.

WhatsApp can be shorter, more casual, and include light emojis. Formal student or parent communications should be organized, reassuring, and informative.

## 5. Your personal voice versus the academy voice

We also discussed an important distinction between you as the founder and Just Keep Talking as a business.

Your personal voice: More conversational, reflective, curious, occasionally funny, and rooted in your experiences as a teacher, language learner, and someone who lives between two languages and cultures.

This is especially valuable for content about English and Spanish comparisons, communication difficulties, cultural differences between Costa Rica and the United States, and things you've observed while teaching.

The academy voice: Still warm and human, but slightly more structured, informative, and focused on the student's experience and the services we provide.

These shouldn't feel like two separate personalities. The academy's voice should feel like a natural extension of yours.

## 6. Bilingual communication

For Spanish content, especially when speaking to a Costa Rican audience, we established these guidelines:

- Use natural, friendly Costa Rican Spanish, generally with usted.
- Adapt messages to sound natural in Spanish rather than translating English word for word.
- Keep the same warmth, clarity, and conversational feeling in both languages.
- Recognize regional and cultural differences without treating one way of speaking as universally correct.
- Use bilingual comparisons when they provide a genuinely interesting insight, not just to fill content.

## 7. What we want people to feel

Across all platforms, our communication should help people feel three things:

Understood

"They understand why learning a language can be difficult and what I'm experiencing."

Confident in our expertise

"These are experienced teachers who understand how language learning works and can help me make progress."

Comfortable and welcome

"This feels like a place where I can practice, make mistakes, ask questions, and be myself."

## 8. Our guiding rule for content

Before publishing something, we should ask:

Does this sound like something we would genuinely say to a student or someone interested in language learning?

And:

Is this helping someone understand something, feel connected, learn something useful, or get to know us better? Or are we just posting because we feel like we should?

We don't need to chase every trend, publish generic tips every day, or turn every post into a sales pitch.

Our content should build trust through real insight, meaningful conversations, personality, and consistent expertise.
