# Audrey Our Story — owner paste

Received 23 October 2026 (America/Guatemala). Use this wording on the Our Story page. Do not LLM-polish.

Audrey studied Spanish throughout her school and university years. Her classes often used traditional methods, and so she practiced grammar and memorized vocabulary as all students do. But when the opportunity to speak to native Spanish speakers presented itself, speaking still didn’t feel natural. Knowing the rules had not given her the confidence to join a conversation.

An exchange program in Costa Rica changed everything. Surrounded by people, culture, and everyday conversations, she began to feel that she could really speak the language. She spoke to locals, she made mistakes, they corrected her and taught her new words. The experience changed how she understood language learning: practice, connection, and meaningful interaction made a difference.

Back in the United States, she continued studying education with a focus on teaching Spanish and English as a second language. In 2021, she moved to Costa Rica, got a few private students, and began creating personalized virtual lessons with a conversational methodology in mind. She didn’t know it at the time, but that is when she started building the academy that became Just Keep Talking.

Her idea was simple: create a place where students want to learn, practice, and speak. A place where they feel comfortable making mistakes and receive constructive feedback and encouragement. That purpose still shapes our approach: lessons built around each learner, real conversations, and supportive relationships that help people work toward their own goals.
