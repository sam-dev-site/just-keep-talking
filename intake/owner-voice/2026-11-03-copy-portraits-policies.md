## How We Teach

We teach through real conversation, with structure, guidance, and feedback to help students develop fluency while receiving the corrections they need to improve. Your teacher introduces new concepts and guides you through interactive activities designed to help you use English more naturally and confidently in real life.

### Real Conversation

Classes are built around authentic conversations and situations you might encounter in everyday life.

### Clear Feedback

Your teacher listens, guides, and provides corrections and feedback to help you improve as you speak.

### All Language Skills

Speaking is at the center of our methodology, but we also work on listening, reading, writing, grammar, pronunciation, and vocabulary to help you develop well-rounded English skills.

### Confidence

One of our main goals is to help you feel more confident and comfortable using English in your everyday life.

### Practical Grammar

We teach grammar explicitly when needed, but always follow it with opportunities to practice. Our goal is for you to not only understand the rules, but also know how to apply them naturally when speaking.

## Certified Native Teachers

Classes are led by experienced, certified native-speaking teachers. During your intake meeting, we help match you with a teacher who fits your goals and schedule.
