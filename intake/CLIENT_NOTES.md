## Business goal

- Create an informational website explaining the program, methodology, and prices.
- Help prospective students discover the business through Google Search.
- Serve as the primary company link on LinkedIn and make the business appear more professional.

## Primary customer

- Working adults who want to improve their English.
- They may have studied English when they were younger but now need stronger English for career advancement.
- They may be recent university graduates who need better English to qualify for higher-paying jobs.

## Visitor goal and primary action

- Visitors should be able to understand the program and get answers to their questions.
- Primary conversion action: schedule an intake interview or contact the business about the intake process.
- Confirmed call to action: send a WhatsApp message to Just Keep Talking at +506 8685 8056.
- Intended WhatsApp destination: `https://wa.me/50686858056`.

## Program and methodology

- One-to-one, personalized, conversation-focused English classes.
- Grammar is included, but it is not the sole or primary emphasis.
- Each student receives a personalized plan based on an intake interview.
- Each student is assigned a teacher and a consistent weekly schedule.
- The teacher helps the student work toward their specific goals.
- The program provides extensive speaking practice.
- The website should especially speak to people who already have a reasonable grammar foundation from earlier programs but still feel afraid to speak English.
- Examples mentioned in the interview included Intensa, Open English, and one unidentified national program. The unidentified name must be clarified before it is used in public copy.
- Classes are 100% virtual through Zoom.
- All classes are private.
- Supplied marketing material states that teachers are native and certified. Qualifications and permission to publish this claim still require confirmation.
- The teaching approach is conversational and reinforces other skills without textbooks.
